## Brand & Style

Pickleball Club Hub conveys a vibrant, athletic, community-driven sports management experience. The aesthetic balances corporate SaaS reliability with modern sports club vitality. It pairs vibrant purple/violet club identity tones with clear slate neutrals and energetic multi-hue skill chips (emerald, amber, cyan, indigo).

### Visual Movements & Style Directives
- **Modern Clean SaaS**: Crisp white cards and containers over a subtle cool background (`slate-50`), structured borders (`slate-200`), and meticulous vertical alignment.
- **Vibrant Gradients**: Expressive high-energy hero headers blending deep violet into indigo (`from-brand-600 via-indigo-600 to-purple-700`) with soft frosted glass elements.
- **Micro-Glassmorphism**: Hero accessory badges and cards use subtle translucent overlays (`white/10` to `white/20`) with backdrop blur (`backdrop-blur-md`) to elevate tactical quick-action prompts without visual clutter.

## Colors

The palette revolves around a sporty, modern royal purple (`#7c3aed`) backed by crisp Slate neutrals (`slate-50` through `slate-900`).

### Role Distribution
- **Primary (`#7c3aed`)**: Highlights active navigation items, key calls-to-action, player profile avatars, progress bars, and pagination active states.
- **Secondary (`#4f46e5`)**: Provides gradient depth across hero banners and accents skill rating bars and metrics.
- **Tertiary / Warning (`#f59e0b`)**: Marks competitive highlights, leaderboards, trophies, and "Hot" feature pills.
- **Success / Activity (`#10b981` / `#047857`)**: Used for online presence pulses, weekly match participation streaks, and active club status indicators.
- **Neutrals (`slate-50` to `slate-900`)**: Structure background canvasses, borders, table stripes, and crisp multi-tier typography.

## Typography

Typography relies uniformly on **Plus Jakarta Sans**, utilizing its geometric clarity, friendly curves, and legibility for data-dense club directories and status views.

- **Headlines**: ExtraBold (`800`) and Bold (`700`) weights with tight tracking (`-0.025em`) provide confidence and impact.
- **Data & Tables**: Regular body text is sized at `0.875rem` (14px) and captions at `0.75rem` (12px), balancing compactness with legibility.
- **Section Dividers & Table Headers**: Uppercase labels at `11px` with `letterSpacing: 0.05em` and bold weights (`600`–`700`) establish clean typographic hierarchy.

## Layout & Spacing

The design adheres to an administrative web app layout:
- **Navigation Shell**: A fixed `16rem` (256px) left sidebar coupled with a persistent `4rem` (64px) top header bar.
- **Main Canvas**: A fluid scrollable viewport constrained within an inner content boundary of `max-w-7xl` (`80rem` / 1280px), centered with `2rem` (32px) padding on desktops and dynamic scaling on mobile.
- **Component Grid & Rhythm**: An 8px/4px structural grid. Filter rows, table rows, and banner blocks maintain `1rem` (16px) internal cell padding and `1.5rem` (24px) vertical spacing gaps.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses flat crisp borders alongside targeted ambient glow shadows:

- **Surface Baselines**: Clean white surfaces outlined with `border border-slate-200/90` over a `slate-50` backdrop.
- **Elevation Layers**:
  - `shadow-xs` / `shadow-sm`: Used on interactive elements (filter toggle tabs, button groups, badge pills) to indicate clickability.
  - `shadow-md` / `shadow-lg` with Color Tints: Used sparingly on primary brand containers and hero banners (e.g., `shadow-brand-500/15` or `shadow-brand-500/20`), creating a purposeful purple luminescence.
- **Overlays**: Translucent frosted panels (`bg-white/10` with `backdrop-blur-md` and `border-white/20`) layered over gradient heroes.

## Shapes

The interface embraces a modern, friendly rounded language:
- **Base Components**: Standard inputs, table actions, and navigation items use `rounded-lg` (8px).
- **Cards, Sidebars & Hero Containers**: Elevated feature wrappers, user status cards, and the main hero header utilize `rounded-xl` (12px) and `rounded-2xl` (16px).
- **Badges & Avatars**: Metrics, DUPR chips, online indicators, and avatars use pill shapes (`rounded-full`) or circular formats.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action**: Violet background (`bg-brand-600 text-white hover:bg-brand-700`), bold font weight, `rounded-lg`, subtle shadow.
- **Subtle / Secondary Action**: Soft brand tint (`bg-brand-50 text-brand-700 border border-brand-200/80 hover:bg-brand-100`).
- **Icon / Utility Buttons**: Square `rounded-lg` buttons with neutral text (`text-slate-400 hover:text-brand-600 hover:bg-slate-100`).

### Filter Chips & Tags
- **Skill & Achievement Chips**: Rounded pills (`rounded-full`) with subtle tint backgrounds and light matching borders:
  - Attendance Streaks: `bg-emerald-50 text-emerald-700 border-emerald-200`.
  - Rankings & Quotas: `bg-amber-50 text-amber-800 border-amber-300`.
  - General Attributes: `bg-slate-100 text-slate-600`.

### Data Table
- **Header**: Light muted slate bar (`bg-slate-50/80 border-b border-slate-200`) with uppercase `11px` typography.
- **Row Interaction**: Hover state shifts to `hover:bg-slate-50/80` with seamless hover transitions (`transition-colors`).
- **Metric Visualizations**: Progress meters for PHR/DUPR ratings use track height of `1.5` (`h-1.5`) in `slate-100` filled with primary purple or indigo bars.

### Input Fields & Switchers
- **Search Bar**: Placed on `bg-slate-50` with inset search glyph, transitioning to pure white with brand ring (`focus:ring-2 focus:ring-brand-500`) on focus.
- **Segmented Controls**: Contained in `bg-slate-100` padding tracks with raised active white tabs (`bg-white shadow-sm font-bold`).
